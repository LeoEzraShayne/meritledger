Merit Ledger FAQs

Quick answers about the app and its official website

What is Merit Ledger?

Merit Ledger is a self-reflection and behavior-tracking app for recording daily positive and negative actions, reviewing patterns, and tracking change over time.

Who is it for?

It is for people who want a structured, private way to build self-awareness, review habits, and support personal growth through regular reflection.

Where can I use it?

Merit Ledger is available as a web application and an Android app. See the download page for access links.

What is the official website?

The official website is official.meritledger.org. It provides feature documentation, guides, contact details, privacy information, and terms.
