Account Deletion

Public page for Google Play account deletion requirement

App:Merit Ledger
Developer:Merit Ledger Team
Last Updated:2026-02-19
Contact:contact@meritledger.org

1. How to delete your account in the app

The delete-account feature is available to signed-in users and can be completed in the app:

  1. Open Merit Ledger and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to "Me / Settings".
  3. Open "Account & Security" (or "Account Management").
  4. Tap "Delete Account".
  5. Complete identity verification and confirm deletion.

2. If you cannot sign in

If you cannot access your account (lost device, sign-in issue, etc.), email contact@meritledger.org with your account email and required verification info. We will assist after identity verification.

3. What data is deleted

  • Account profile and authentication linkage are deleted or anonymized.
  • Cloud-synced records associated with the account are deleted.
  • Local data on your device is not automatically removed by remote account deletion.

4. Data retention

  • After request submission and verification, deletion is processed within a reasonable period.
  • Minimum necessary logs may be retained for legal, security, and dispute handling, then deleted or anonymized.

5. Link for Google Play

Official public URL for Google Play: https://official.meritledger.org/delete-account
